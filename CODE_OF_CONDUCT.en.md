# Starbit Community Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

The Starbit community is committed to an open, welcoming, professional, and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, physical appearance, religion, sexual orientation, socioeconomic status, or any other personal characteristic.

By participating, you agree to follow this code and help maintain a community where reasoned disagreement is welcome, evidence matters, and personal boundaries are respected.

## Expected behavior

- Use welcoming, accurate, and inclusive language.
- Discuss code, design, tests, and verifiable facts without attacking people.
- Respect differences in background, language, and experience, and provide necessary context patiently.
- Accept constructive feedback, acknowledge mistakes, and correct behavior that may cause harm.
- Disclose conflicts of interest that could affect your judgment.
- Handle logs, screenshots, crash dumps, prompts, and configuration carefully; remove credentials, personal data, and other secrets before sharing them.
- Respect maintainer decisions concerning security, releases, and repository boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, stalking, threats, or personal attacks.
- Insulting, sexualized, or violent content, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing another person's private information, credentials, undisclosed vulnerabilities, or private communications.
- Intentionally contributing malicious code, backdoors, dependency poisoning, prompt-injection payloads, or destructive test data.
- Impersonation, review manipulation, spam, or sustained disruption of project work.
- Continuing inappropriate behavior after being asked to stop.
- Treating reasonable technical criticism as a personal attack, or using this code to suppress good-faith technical disagreement.

## Scope

This code applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions, release channels, and other online or offline spaces where someone represents Starbit. Private conduct may also fall within scope when it directly affects participant safety or the project's ability to operate.

## Reporting and response

Report conduct concerns privately through a contact method listed on the repository owner's profile. Do not disclose a person's private information in a public issue. Report security vulnerabilities through the private process in [SECURITY.en.md](SECURITY.en.md).

A useful report includes when and where the incident occurred, relevant links or screenshots, who was affected, and the outcome requested. Maintainers will limit disclosure, respect the reporter's privacy, and investigate without creating additional harm. Deliberately false or abusive reports may themselves violate this code.

## Enforcement principles

Maintainers will choose a proportionate response based on the nature, impact, duration, and history of the behavior. Responses may include a private correction, formal warning, interaction restrictions, temporary suspension, permanent removal, or contacting the platform or relevant authorities when there is a credible risk of physical harm. Maintainers should identify the applicable rule and corrective expectation, but may withhold sensitive details to protect those involved.

Every participant, including every maintainer, is subject to the same standard. A person who disputes a decision may request review through the same private channel by a maintainer who was not directly involved. If the project has only one maintainer, that maintainer will reconsider the decision when new evidence is provided.

## Version

This code takes effect on September 4, 2026. Material revisions will remain visible in the repository history.
